Traced, not sketched

Version 1 guessed the petal shape and got it wrong. This version decodes emanuel_logo.png and measures the real silhouette in polar coordinates, then rebuilds it as vector geometry. Direction is now clockwise, and adjustable below.

What changed since v1
  • Petal is geometric. Circular head, pinched neck, hard horizontal shoulder, straight‑sided tapering wedge, pointed foot. v1 had a soft paisley with curved sides.
  • The white "eye" is gone. I invented it; the trace shows the head is solid.
  • Ornament band is real. Three keystones per petal, one on‑axis and two flanking at ±8°, at radius 35–40 of 50. v1 had one dot in the wrong place.
  • Rotation is clockwise by default, and there is a toggle.
  • A and B are fused. The tracery arc and the petal beam run phase‑locked, so the band reads as the light source and the petals as what it falls on.
  • Resting opacity raised from .14 to .40, and the band from .26 to .50. Saturated colour on white goes pastel fast; below about .35 the window read as washed‑out watercolour rather than as the mark. These are the values that shipped.

A + B, fused

The arc and the beam are phase‑locked: same angular rate, with the dash's leading edge sitting exactly on the brightest petal. Without that lock they read as two unrelated things moving. The band carries the light in at the rim; the petals wash bright just behind it.

Illumination with tracery lead

Moves: an arc on the band, and the light it casts across the petals

The petal geometry never rotates — only its illumination travels, so the hexagram stays upright and still. The arc is the one genuinely rotating element, and it reads as the source of the sweep rather than as a separate spinner.

Cost: twelve opacity animations plus one transform. No layout properties animated, so it stays on the compositor.

The two ingredients, separately

A · Illumination alone

Beam only, band static. Quiet, but at small sizes it can read as flickering rather than turning.

B · Tracery arc alone

Arc only, petals static and dim. Unambiguously a spinner, but the window is reduced to decoration behind it.

C · Cascade (not selected)

Discrete twelve‑step tick. Kept here only for comparison.

Size tiers

Detail sheds as the mark shrinks, and below 32px the rose gives way entirely to a conic ring in the same six hues. Twelve petals plus a hexagram plus thirty‑six keystones do not resolve at 20px, and pretending otherwise just yields a coloured smudge.
