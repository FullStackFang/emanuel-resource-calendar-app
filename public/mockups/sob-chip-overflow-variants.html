A · single-row scroll strip (recommended)

One row, always. Full-size chips scroll horizontally; the selected chip is auto-scrolled into view, so the conflict stepper doubles as the navigator (each step scrolls the strip). Edge fades signal overflow. 20 or 200 occurrences take the same vertical space, and the temporal shape survives as a scrollable timeline. No new state, no pagination logic.

Chips for the current two-week window only; arrows page through the series and the range label says where you are. Compact, but it adds a second navigation axis (window pager + conflict stepper), and conflicts outside the window are invisible until you page to them or use the stepper, which must also jump windows.

Above a threshold (say 14 occurrences) only conflicted chips render; clear dates collapse behind 'Show all'. An all-clear series (second panel) collapses to one line, since the verdict already says everything. Minimal space, triage-first, but browsing a specific clear date takes an extra click and the temporal shape of the series disappears.
